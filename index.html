About

I'm a Data Scientist with experience building predictive models, GenAI workflows, data pipelines, and business-facing analytics solutions. I enjoy working across the full data science lifecycle—from understanding a messy question and exploring the data to validating a model and helping people use the result.

At RaceTrac, I work across loyalty data modernization, Databricks/PySpark pipelines, AI/BI self-service analytics, churn modeling, and Azure AI Foundry agent workflows. The common thread is simple: turn complex business questions into practical, trustworthy solutions people can actually use.
